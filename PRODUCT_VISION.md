# SLEDSS — Sensor-led healthy ageing

SLEDSS turns fragmented lifestyle tests and future sensor readings into understandable, cautious, actionable guidance for older adults.

## Intelligence model

1. **Measure:** ingest readings with device, time, unit, firmware and signal-quality provenance.
2. **Validate:** reject implausible values, stale timestamps and low-quality signals.
3. **Personalize:** compare trends with the person's baseline; do not diagnose from a single reading.
4. **Triangulate:** combine sensors with validated sleep, nutrition, stress, activity, social and digital-literacy assessments.
5. **Explain:** show which evidence led to each insight and its confidence.
6. **Act:** recommend one small, achievable next step and track whether it helped.
7. **Escalate safely:** distinguish everyday wellness nudges from symptoms that need professional or urgent care.

## Fundable roadmap

- Pilot: usability, accessibility, retention and sensor-data completeness with older adults.
- Validation: prospective study comparing SLEDSS risk flags with clinician-reviewed outcomes.
- Integration: FHIR-compatible summaries, consented caregiver sharing and device adapters.
- Scale: multilingual, low-bandwidth/offline capture, community-health-worker mode and population dashboards using de-identified aggregates.

SLEDSS should be positioned as a decision-support and behaviour-change platform until clinical claims have been independently validated and the applicable medical-device requirements are met.
